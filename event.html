Past Events

Explore themes and directors across TEDxUQ’s history.

2023 Small Ripples, Big Waves

Directors: Ally Fulton, Madalyn Conwell, and Yan Yee Lee

The theme highlights that even when something seems small, it can have a resounding impact long into the future. From 6 speakers and 100 attendees in 2013 to a tenth anniversary event welcoming 12 speakers and 500 attendees, TEDxUQ celebrates the ripple effect of ideas.

2022 Challengers. Changemakers. Champions

Directors: Ally Fulton and Madalyn Conwell

TEDxUQ 2022 celebrated speakers who challenge the status quo, create change, and champion their fields—prompting us to reimagine our worldview.

2021 Connected

Directors: Janice Reid and Madalyn Conwell

In 2021, TEDxUQ chose the theme Connected to celebrate the opportunity to come together, especially in light of the COVID-19 pandemic.

2019 Ask Why

Directors: Janice Reid and Madalyn Conwell

Ask Why celebrated curiosity and challenging what we think we know. It was TEDxUQ’s largest event to date, with 500 attendees gathering in the Advanced Engineering Building.

2018 i am [ ]

Directors: Janice Reid and Jarrad Aspen

This theme encapsulated two key pillars—innovation and diversity—and explored how people define themselves through lived experience.

2017 Thinking Out Loud

Director: Adrian Stokes

Conversations and collaboration as the driving force behind innovation and change, with a diverse range of domains including science, technology, psychology, business, and culture.

2016 Future Frontiers

Director: Montgomery Quaife-Ryan

A theme shaped by uncertainty and a desire to uncover new frontiers across science, technology, psychology, business, law, and the arts.

2015 Rewrite the Rules

Director: Marya Atmeh

TEDxUQ’s third annual instalment, focused on how our thoughts and understandings are shaped by tradition, upbringing, and external factors.

2014 Old Ideas, New World

Director: Lawrence Kurniawan Wong

Exploring how connecting with classic ideas can open a world of possibilities in an age of exponential change.

2013 Extraordinary

Director: Lawrence Kurniawan Wong

TEDxUQ’s inaugural event at the Physiology Lecture Theatre, featuring a diverse range of topics including human minds, robots, impostors, success, genetically modified organisms, and entrepreneurship.
